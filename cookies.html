    Cookies Policy

    How Dalmere Form Theory uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your reading experience.

    Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective immediately

    This Cookies Policy explains how Dalmere Form Theory ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website at www.dalmereformtheory.com (the "Site").

    It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

    By continuing to browse the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you should adjust your browser settings or discontinue use of the Site.

    In short: We use a small number of cookies to keep the Site running smoothly, understand how readers use it, and remember your preferences. You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies may be session cookies (which are deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

    We also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage, which function in comparable ways. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential cookies — necessary for the Site to function properly, such as remembering your cookie preferences or enabling secure areas
    • Analytics cookies — help us understand how readers interact with the Site, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve our content and navigation
    • Preference cookies — remember choices you make, such as your preferred reading settings or language
    • Performance cookies — collect information about how the Site performs, including loading times and any errors, so we can fix them

    We do not use cookies for advertising or retargeting purposes.

    3. Specific Cookies We Use

    Below is a list of the main cookies we use and their purposes:

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    dft_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences 1 year
    dft_session Essential Maintains your session while browsing Session
    _ga Analytics Google Analytics — distinguishes unique users 2 years
    _gid Analytics Google Analytics — distinguishes users 24 hours
    _gat Analytics Google Analytics — throttles request rate 1 minute
    dft_prefs Preference Remembers your reading preferences 6 months

    Third-party cookies (such as Google Analytics) are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties. We recommend reviewing their policies for more information.

    4. Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies on our Site are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. The main third-party cookies we use are:

    • Google Analytics — for understanding site traffic and reader behaviour. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
    • Font providers — Google Fonts may set cookies when loading typography. These are typically essential for displaying the Site correctly.

    We do not use social media cookies, advertising cookies, or any other tracking technology for commercial purposes.

    5. How to Manage Cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

    • Browser settings — most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. The help function in your browser should tell you how.
    • Our cookie banner — when you first visit the Site, you can choose which categories of cookies to accept.
    • Opt-out tools — for analytics cookies, you can use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

    Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the Site may become inaccessible or not function properly. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent tool, as they are necessary for the Site to operate.

    5.1 Browser-Specific Instructions

    • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
    • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
    • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
    • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that lets you tell websites you do not want to be tracked. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. We do, however, respect your cookie preferences as set through our consent tool and browser settings.

    7. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will:

    • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
    • Post a notice on the Site where appropriate
    • Where required by law, seek your consent again

    We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
